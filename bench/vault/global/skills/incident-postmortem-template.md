# Incident postmortem template

1. **Summary** — one paragraph, no jargon.
2. **Timeline** — UTC, from first signal to resolution.
3. **Impact** — who was affected, for how long, money involved.
4. **Root cause** — technical cause and why it was possible.
5. **What went well / badly**.
6. **Follow-ups** — each with an owner and a ticket; nothing without an owner.

Blameless: describe systems, not people.
