# Onboard an organizer

## Steps
1. Admin console → Organizers → New: legal name, VAT number, contact.
2. Create the Paylane connected account and complete the identity check.
3. Enter the bank details for payouts; Marta approves the first transfer.
4. Invite the organizer's staff to the dashboard with the "manager" role.
5. Give Gatekeeper devices to the venue: one token per venue ([[tidewater/plans/2026-03-22-jwt-auth]]).
