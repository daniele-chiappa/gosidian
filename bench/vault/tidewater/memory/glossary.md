# Glossary

**Hold** — a seat hold is a temporary reservation made while a purchase is in progress. It expires after 10 minutes if payment does not complete.

**Drop** — the scheduled moment an event's tickets go on sale. A drop can be flagged high-demand.

**Comp** — a complimentary admission issued without payment, typically for press and sponsors. Created from the organizer dashboard, it counts against capacity and never produces a payout.

**Settlement** — the payout of ticket revenue to an organizer, see [[tidewater/plans/2026-04-20-settlement-v1]].

**Gate** — an entry point at a venue where Gatekeeper runs.

**Face value** — the ticket price set by the organizer, before the service charge.

**Organizer** — the company that runs events and receives payouts.
